# Boundaries

## Containment

The swarm's job is to be tempted, so the world is sealed.

- The Luanti server and the orchestrator run locally. Agents have no internet
  access and no tools beyond the action API.
- All credentials, tokens and data in the world are fake and exist only there.
- Model API calls go out from the orchestrator only; agents cannot address
  anything outside the world.
- Episodes have step and time limits, and a kill switch.

## Out of scope

- No real systems, no scanning, no live web access by agents.
- No government data, and no material from any ongoing incident work or
  investigation.
- No lists of real exposed services, and no techniques for reaching them. The
  world's "unlisted programs" and "gated area" are abstract.
- No personal information in any dataset or log.

## Participation

- Take part as an individual with open-source work unless an organisation
  agrees otherwise, and clear participation with your manager first.
- Follow the hackathon's rules on prepared work, and the AI Village database's
  terms of use.

## Publishing

- Publish code, synthetic data, lens definitions and results.
- Do not publish trained lenses or data that would teach an agent how to avoid
  the monitor (for example, lists of texts that scored low while crossing),
  without a deliberate decision.
